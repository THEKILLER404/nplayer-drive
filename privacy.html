Privacy Policy — nPlayer Drive

What this is: nPlayer Drive is a personal-use project that lets the nPlayer Android app access your own Google Drive files through an OAuth client you created in your own Google Cloud project.

Data collected: Nothing. There is no server, no database, and no analytics. The OAuth tokens live only on your own Android device, inside the nPlayer app.

Google Drive API use: The app requests Google Drive access solely to list and stream files from the Google account you sign in with. Your files are never copied, shared, or sent anywhere except between Google's servers and your device.

Sharing: No data is shared with any third party, because no data leaves your device apart from the normal Google Drive API calls you authorize.

Contact: This is a single-user personal project. For questions, contact the project owner through the Google account used to create the Cloud project.
