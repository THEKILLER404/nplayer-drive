Privacy Policy

This is a personal-use project for testing Google Drive login and browsing files.

Data collected: Nothing. There is no server, no database, and no analytics. Any sign-in tokens stay on your own device.

Google API use: Google APIs are used only to test sign-in and browse files for the account you sign in with. Files are never copied or shared anywhere.

Sharing: No data is shared with any third party.
